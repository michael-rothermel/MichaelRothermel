Approach

Editing is what happens in the overlap.

Put two images side by side and the audience creates a third: a feeling neither shot holds alone. That's the part of the job I love. A good story can change you, and my work is finding that story in the footage.

My work has appeared on PBS, Universal and SXSW. In 2021 I co-founded CHAIYO, a studio for stories that spark empathy, and I take on freelance edits with directors, producers and agencies who care about the same thing.
